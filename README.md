# Financial CSI 

An AI-powered Risk Manager that discovers, explains, and bounds potentially preventable payment loss through emergent pattern mining and counterfactual simulation.

## Problem
Traditional risk and fraud systems rely on static, predefined rules (e.g., "Block IP if failures > 5" or "Decline if amount > $500"). However, modern payment ecosystems are highly complex. Material merchant loss often emerges not from simple brute-force attacks, but from the nuanced interaction of technical failures: edge-case gateway timeouts, aggressive retry cascades, and delayed webhooks resulting in unfulfilled inventory or duplicate captures. 

## Solution
Financial CSI shifts the paradigm from "Deterministic Rules" to "Emergent Discovery":
1. It ingests complex payment lifecycles and reconstructs them into categorical **Financial DNA**.
2. It statistically mines combinations of this DNA to find unspecified event combinations disproportionately associated with financial loss.
3. It grounds LLM AI explanations explicitly in underlying event evidence to prevent hallucination.
4. It executes Monte Carlo counterfactual simulations to determine if intervention is economically viable after calculating False-Positive friction costs.

## What is Novel?
**Emergent Pattern Discovery**: Rather than relying exclusively on a team of analysts manually hypothesizing fraud vectors and writing predefined baseline risk rules, Financial CSI organically mines the interaction topology of the event data to find combinations that human operators didn't specify. 

## AI Role
AI is utilized purely for **Evidence-Grounded Investigation**. It does not perform black-box anomaly detection. Instead, it consumes deterministic `EvidencePacks` (containing raw timestamps, identifiers, and supports) generated by the Apriori pattern miner, translating complex statistical outputs into actionable forensic briefs for human operators.

## Evaluation & Safety
- **Strict Data Holdout**: The Train/Test split is mathematically isolated. Leakage checks explicitly prevent overlapping identifiers, and discovered patterns are validated against the unseen Test split.
- **Financial Simulation**: Savings are exclusively presented as "potentially preventable loss under simulation assumptions," bounded by explicit intervention efficacies (e.g. 60%, 75%, 90%).
- **No Live Financial Action**: The system acts strictly as an advisory Simulator. The Razorpay integration uses Test Mode webhooks via authenticated HMAC-SHA256 signature verification to safely ingest event sequences without production modification.

## Limitations
- **Synthetic Data**: The primary benchmark rests on deterministically generated synthetic data simulating complex failures. Real-world implementation requires production ML hyperparameter tuning.
- **Correlation ≠ Causality**: The discovery engine identifies statistical risk-multipliers. It does not definitively prove the anomaly caused the loss.
- **Simulation Assumptions**: Calculated Net Estimated Benefits assume the merchant can effectively enact the selected intervention with the provided efficacy success rate without unexpected side effects.
